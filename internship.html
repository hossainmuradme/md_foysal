Practical training

Internship & clinical exposure

Original internship photographs and documentation from Foysal’s Google Sites print.

Placement 01

Central Disease Investigation Laboratory

Molecular and immunodiagnostic techniques including PCR/RT-PCR, ELISA, microbiology, bacteriology and cell culture, with emphasis on biosafety.

Placement 02

Central Veterinary Hospital / Small-animal clinical care

Exposure to examinations, ultrasound, radiography, ophthalmology, anesthesia and common surgical procedures in dogs and cats.

Placement 03

Livestock Research Institute (LRI)

Training associated with vaccine production, seed/media culture and vaccine quality-control processes, including sterility, safety and efficacy tests.

Placement 04

Bangladesh Livestock Research Institute (BLRI)

Research laboratory exposure involving vaccines, animal production, culture and analytical procedures.

Placement 05

Central Cattle Breeding and Dairy Farm

Bull breeding and semen production, veterinary disease management, dairy milk pasteurization and feed management.
